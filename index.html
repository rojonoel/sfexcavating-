About us

Our Story

At SR Excavating, we’re a small, family-owned operation built on a deep respect for hard work and honest service. What started as a few pieces of equipment and a strong work ethic has grown into a trusted name for underground utility work across Denver and the surrounding region.

Our roots are in excavation — moving earth, breaking concrete, solving problems underground — but over time, we’ve grown to offer a full range of services, including:

  • Water & Sewer Line Work
  • Interior Jackhammering
  • Full Line Replacements
  • Tap & Sprinkler Line Repairs

We take pride in being hands-on. When you work with us, you’re working directly with the people who own the company — people who live here, raise their families here, and treat every job like it’s in their own backyard.
